Sweet Spot Ranges

Evidence-based ranges for active individuals building or maintaining lean muscle. These are starting points — adjust based on results over 3–4 weeks.

Protein 1.6–2.2g / kg LBM

Hitting Your Targets

Practical adds, ranked by impact, relevant to what's in this collection.

Protein

Typical shortfall: rare if using specified amounts. Most likely gap is breakfast.
  • Chicken breast — 31g/100g, leanest option per gram
  • Shrimp — 24g/100g, extremely lean, 2–3 min cook time
  • Canned tuna — ~25g per can, shelf-stable, zero prep
  • Greek yogurt 0% — 10g/100g; works as sauce base, marinade, or breakfast
  • Eggs — ~6.5g each; fast and cheap; works as a topper on most dishes
  • Whey protein — 24g/scoop; useful if breakfast is low-protein
Hitting 160–180g protein/day is realistic across 3 meals at the recipe amounts. The gap is usually breakfast — a smoothie with Greek yogurt and protein powder closes it fast.

Fat

Typical shortfall: real. Chicken breast days can land at 25–40g total without deliberate adds.
  • Salmon — 13g/100g, omega-3-rich; best fat source in the collection
  • Peanut butter — ~16g fat per 2 tbsp, mostly monounsaturated
  • Avocado — ~15g fat per 100g; pairs with Mexican dishes naturally
  • Sesame oil — ~4.5g/tsp as a finishing drizzle on any Korean/Japanese dish
  • Eggs — ~5g fat each (all in the yolk)
  • Olive oil — best for cooking and finishing Italian dishes
  • Full-fat vs 0% Greek yogurt — switching adds ~5–8g fat per serving
The goal isn't to chase fat — it's to not chronically avoid it. The minimum (0.5g/kg bodyweight) exists for hormonal reasons, not aesthetics. If you're eating chicken breast for 2–3 meals a day, you need deliberate fat sources.

Carbs

Typical shortfall: not a problem. Rice, pasta, and noodles handle it automatically.
  • White rice — ~80g carbs / 100g dry; high GI, fast energy
  • Pasta / soba — ~71g carbs / 100g dry; lower GI than rice
  • Oats — ~60g carbs / 100g; good breakfast base, slow-digesting
  • Sweet potato — ~20g carbs / 100g; high fiber, good with bowls
If intentionally reducing carbs: sub cauliflower rice in bowls, use lettuce wraps for Mexican dishes, skip noodles in tom yum. The collection works low-carb — just swap the base.
